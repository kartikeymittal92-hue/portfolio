About

The story of a curious mind

I'm Kartikey Mittal — a first-semester B.Tech student at Newton School of Technology (NST-RU). I'm at the very beginning of my journey into technology, and honestly, that's the part I find most exciting.

Every day is a blank page. I'm writing my first Python scripts, learning my way around the terminal, and slowly figuring out how this vast world of code fits together. I don't have all the answers yet — but I have all the questions, and that feels like a good start.

"The best way to predict the future is to start building it — one curious experiment at a time."

Outside of code, I'm the person planning the next trip, researching hidden food spots, or just sitting somewhere beautiful, soaking it all in. I think living fully and building things are two sides of the same coin.
